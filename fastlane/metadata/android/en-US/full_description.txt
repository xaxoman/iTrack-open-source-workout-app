iTrack is a privacy-focused, open-source workout tracking application that helps you maintain consistency in your fitness journey.

Features:
• Create custom workout routines with detailed exercises
• Track your workout in real time: tick off every exercise, set by set
• Large demo videos or GIFs above the current exercise
• Repetition and time-based exercises, with a built-in timer
• Rest timer with sounds bundled in the app, so they work offline
• Target muscle group tracking
• Calendar history: open, edit or delete past workouts
• Stats, weekly trends and records for every routine
• Body-weight log in kg or lb
• Available in English and Italian
• No trackers, no ads, and completely free

iTrack is designed to be simple, effective, and privacy-respecting. Your workout data stays on your device - we don't collect any data or require internet access for core functionality.

This app is open-source and community-driven. Contributions are welcome on GitHub!
